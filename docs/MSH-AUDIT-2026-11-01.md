## Independent pass · 3 November 2026 (supersedes the verdict above)

**Builder:** Claude (fixes). **Auditor:** a separate reviewer agent, which read the production screenshots blind with the B1–B14 checklist. The builder did not score its own work.
**Live build:** the release after commit fa2de5e. **Evidence:** `docs/evidence/msh-2026-11-03/` (5 screenshots, `geometry.json`, `menu.json`), rendered by headless Chrome against production.

### Corrections to the 1 November audit
- **B9 "map ≈94%" was not measured.** The real phone chrome on 1 Nov was header 48 + watch 141 + map key 66 px, about 30% of the viewport. The 2 Nov rebuild by another agent and this pass bring it to 11–12% on the phone and 8% on tablet and desktop.
- **§11.7 was not checked.** Six text sizes were rendering. There are now exactly three (24 / 13 / 11 px tokens), guarded in `test-anti-slop-design.mjs`.
- **Splash:** an early measurement here (13–22 s, plus a false "Map unavailable") came from a backgrounded browser pane and is withdrawn. Unthrottled production took 1.4–4.8 s before this release. The real defect was the gating: `map.loaded()`/`isStyleLoaded()` wait for every tile, so a slow phone link could still reach the 12 s failure screen. The splash now lifts on `style.load` and failure needs a style that never loaded (guarded and mutation-checked). Now 0.7–3.1 s.
- **Kinetic grids (3 Nov AGENTS.md):** the watch strip grid (`repeat(4, auto)` / `repeat(4, 1fr)`) was unnamed, yet the wind arrow rotates (`rotate(${windTo}deg)`). Per the 3 Nov Kinetic grids companion law, a named grid (`radial` | `axial` | `column` | `modular`) must be declared before any rotation; without a name, rotation is banned. The grid is now documented as `axial` — four equal readings along a horizontal axis — in both `public/style.css` and `public/dhaka-design.css`. The wind arrow rotation is bounded by this named grid. No other geometric rotations exist in the codebase.

### Fixed in this pass
- Watch: emoji replaced with glyphs that take the tier colour. AQI and wind carry "model". Wind is no longer a fake green "ok" (it had no threshold). Values use Bengali digits in bn. The "বাতুঁ" typo is fixed and the en mode no longer shows Bengali labels. Unavailable cells say "n/a / অনুপলব্ধ" (tapping opens the desk with the reason). The desktop strip fits its readings instead of cutting them off.
- Header: identity and lens on the left, language and menu on the right.
- The phone menu clears the watch strip (menu 44–644 px, watch from 749 px); menu labels are left-aligned.
- Phone: the Esri / Maxar / MapLibre imagery credit is no longer covered by the watch strip.
- Watch strip grid named `axial` per Kinetic grids law; wind arrow rotation bounded by the named grid.

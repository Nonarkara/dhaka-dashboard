Night lights — the sensor handover
==================================

Captured 2026-11-20 against https://dhaka.nonarkara.org.

What happened
-------------
NASA stopped publishing the Suomi NPP Day/Night Band on 2026-11-02. The GIBS
capabilities document ends that layer's time dimension there; later dates
answer 404 and the final date serves the blank placeholder GIBS ships before
an ingest. The atlas kept naming the dead product, and the cost was paid in
silence:

  - the map's night lens was blank — `unavailable · gibs-no-observation`;
  - the economy desk published a night from 2026-10-27 as `status: ok`,
    three weeks old, because the tile behind that date still carried data and
    the collector measured whatever it found.

The release gate refused the deploy on this, correctly. The gate was right
and the atlas was wrong.

environment-live.json is the live response, captured after the handover.
gibs-live-verification.txt is the independent decode of every advertised tile:
the night layer now answers 62,921 bytes of imagery, where the dead product's
last tile answered 1,611 bytes of blank placeholder.

The economy desk, before and after
----------------------------------
Before, the live collage carried:
  VIIRS_SNPP_DayNightBand_At_Sensor_Radiance | ok | 32.656 nW/(cm² sr) | 2026-10-27
After, it carries:
  VIIRS_NOAA20_DayNightBand_At_Sensor_Radiance | ok | 13.804 nW/(cm² sr) | 2026-11-18

The night is two decades fresher and it is a different satellite's reading.
The series is not spliced: the readings before the handover stay under the
Suomi NPP layer id in the stored history, and the readings after it carry the
NOAA-20 one. A reader can tell which satellite produced which row.

Why the values are not comparable across the handover
-----------------------------------------------------
They are two satellites' measurements of the same quantity, on the same orbit,
with the same colour map and the same units — but not one continuous series.
The atlas therefore refuses to publish a reading that has aged past four days
(`stale-observation`, with the date it refused) instead of passing an old
night off as tonight's, and it publishes a reading only when the catalogue
says the product is still flying.

The gate
---------
scripts/test-environment-contract.mjs now measures each layer's last
observation against TODAY, not against the catalogue's own capture date: a
product that died after the catalogue was taken is as dead as one that had
already died when it was taken. The measured layers — the registry that feeds
numbers, which was never covered — get the same rule, and the Suomi NPP ids
are in the retired list so no registry may name them again.

Provenance
----------
NASA GIBS WMTS, no key. Night radiance: VIIRS NOAA-20 Day/Night Band at
sensor, Level 8, nW/(cm² sr), colour map
VIIRS_DayNightBand_At_Sensor_Radiance.xml, sampled over a five-pixel window
(about 2.8 km over Dhaka) around each district centre — a sampled window, not
a district average.
